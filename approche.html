frequently asked

What we get asked most

Do we have to fire our agency?
No, and it's rarely a good idea. Most clients keep their agency for creative and day-to-day work, and bring only the technical side in-house.
Are we too small for this?
It's about ad budget, not company size. Below a certain spend this costs more than it returns — and we'll say so rather than sell you an engagement.
Nobody here is technical. A problem?
No — it's the usual situation, and why training is part of the work. Translating is our job, not your job to guess.
What does it cost?
A fixed fee per stage, agreed in advance — never a percentage of your ad spend. We quote after the assessment. The first conversation is free.
