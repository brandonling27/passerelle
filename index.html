in plain terms

The problem, without the jargon

Plenty of brands want to stop outsourcing everything to an agency. The obstacle is almost never the strategy — it is the plumbing underneath it.

01

You pay without seeing where the money goes

Between your budget and the ad someone actually sees, there is a long chain of intermediaries. When you do not control the tools, you cannot check what happens in the middle.

02

The tools do not talk to each other

Your advertising platforms, your website and your customer data all have to be wired together correctly. Wired badly, you pay to reach people you have no interest in.

03

The law changed; your settings did not

Consent, GDPR, the end of advertising cookies: many setups still run on settings that are no longer compliant — and that gets fixed in the tools, not in a legal memo.
