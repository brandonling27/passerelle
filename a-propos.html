the name

Why "Passerelle"

A passerelle is a footbridge: it joins two banks and is crossed in both directions. On one side, marketing teams who know what they want to achieve; on the other, technical machinery speaking an impenetrable dialect. Our job is to walk people across — then let them cross on their own.
