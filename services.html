How we help

You won't need all of it. Most engagements start with one or two areas. The technical term is under each, in case your provider uses it.

01 — setup

Setting up your operation

We pick the platforms that fit your budget, install them and configure them.

also called: DSP selection, trading desk architecture
02 — connections

Getting your tools to talk

Wired wrong, you pay more for a worse result — and nothing flags the mistake.

also called: SSP/DSP integration, header bidding, bidstream
03 — compliance

Staying on the right side of the law

Legal questions with technical answers. We handle the technical half, with your lawyers.

also called: CMP integration, GDPR compliance, cookieless identity
04 — data

Putting your customer data to work

Usable in your campaigns — lawfully, and without handing it to third parties.

also called: first-party data activation, data layer audit
05 — training

Training your team

So they run it without us. This is the part that lets an engagement end.

also called: upskilling, knowledge transfer
06 — ongoing support

Answering questions afterwards

Should we buy this tool? Is this number normal? We stay reachable.

also called: technical advisory, build-vs-buy guidance
worth knowing

What we don't do

We don't run your campaigns, make your creative, or buy ad space for you. We build the machinery; your team — or the agency you keep — uses it.
