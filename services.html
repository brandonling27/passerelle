Six ways we help

You do not need all six. Most engagements start with one or two, and the rest follow only if they are useful. Each one is described in everyday language, with the technical term underneath in case your provider uses it.

01 — setup

Setting up your operation

You want to buy your advertising yourself rather than hand everything to an agency. We choose the platforms that fit your size and budget, install them and configure them.

also called: DSP selection, trading desk architecture
02 — connections

Getting your tools to talk to each other

Your platforms have to exchange the right information in the right order. When those connections are wrong, you pay more for a worse result — and nothing flags the mistake.

also called: SSP/DSP integration, header bidding, bidstream
03 — compliance

Staying on the right side of the law

Consent banners, GDPR, regulator audits, the disappearance of advertising cookies. These are legal questions with technical answers: we handle the technical half, alongside your lawyers.

also called: CMP integration, GDPR compliance, cookieless identity
04 — data

Putting your own customer data to work

You already know a great deal about your customers. We make that information usable in your campaigns — lawfully, and without handing it to third parties who have no business receiving it.

also called: first-party data activation, data layer audit
05 — training

Training your team

Structured training pitched at your team’s actual level, so they can run the setup on their own once we leave. This is the part that lets an engagement genuinely end.

also called: upskilling, knowledge transfer
06 — ongoing support

Answering your questions afterwards

Once everything is in place, questions come up: should we buy this tool? is this offer credible? is this number normal? We stay reachable to answer them.

also called: technical advisory, build-vs-buy guidance
worth knowing

What we do not do

We do not run your campaigns day to day, we do not make your creative, and we do not buy ad space on your behalf. We build the machinery and teach you to operate it; your team — or the agency you keep — uses it afterwards. If what you need is day-to-day management rather than setup, we will tell you in the first conversation.

Not sure which box you are in?

That is the most common situation. Describe it in your own words — working out the right starting point is part of the job.
